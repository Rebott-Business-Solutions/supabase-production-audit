# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't tolerated.

A few points matter especially here:

- Never post anything that identifies a real project, company or person's system, including in a "this is what I found" issue.
- Don't use this tool, or anything learned here, against a system you're not authorised to test.

To report a conduct problem, use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/Rebott-Business-Solutions/supabase-production-audit/security/advisories/new) and say it's about conduct. The maintainers will review it and respond, and they may remove content or block contributors who break these rules.
